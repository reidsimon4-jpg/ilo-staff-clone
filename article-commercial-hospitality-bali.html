Steel Frame for Cafés, Retail and Small Hotels in Bali

What changes when the building is not a villa.

Bali is not only villas. Cafés, shops, co-working spaces, offices and small hotels are built here every year, and owners of these buildings care about one thing more than most: how soon the doors can open. This guide explains how a light gauge steel (LGS) frame suits commercial and hospitality buildings, and what to ask a builder. It does not replace your engineer, who designs each building for its own loads and use.

What ILO builds besides villas

ILO builds luxury residential villas, multi-family townhouses, resort accommodation and commercial buildings. Commercial work includes warehouses, offices, retail spaces and hospitality buildings. The frames are made in the ILO factory in Banyuwangi, Java, and travel to Bali or Lombok. For a plain-English view of the system, see what is light gauge steel.

Why speed matters more for a business

A villa owner waits for a home. A café or hotel owner waits for income. Rent, loans and staff costs often run while the building is still a site. Projects are typically completed 2 to 3 times faster than traditional methods, and a superstructure that takes months in concrete can be erected in days. That can bring the opening date forward. We do not quote fixed weeks here, because the programme depends on size, finish level and site. The usual sequence is in From factory to keys: the ILO build timeline.

Open floor plans and large spans

Shops, cafés and offices usually want open rooms with few columns. ILO describes large-span capability for commercial buildings. How far a frame can span without support depends on the design, so your engineer confirms spans and loads. Roof frames are covered in steel roof trusses for Bali villas, and the same truss thinking applies to larger roofs.

More than one storey

ILO states that LGS can support mid-rise buildings up to 10 storeys, and handles the 2 to 3 storey builds that are common in Bali. Commercial floors often carry heavier loads than a bedroom: people, furniture, kitchens and equipment. Each multi-storey job is engineered separately. Read more in two-storey steel frame villas in Bali and steel frames for multi-villa developments.

Humidity, termites and busy kitchens

Commercial buildings in Bali face the same climate as villas. LGS is termite proof and rust resistant, and steel is fire resistant, which matters where kitchens and many guests are involved. Fire and safety rules for commercial use are set by the authorities and your engineer, so ask your builder what applies to your building. The tropical details are in termites, humidity and rust: how steel frames hold up in the tropics.

Less disruption for neighbours and guests

Many commercial sites in Bali sit in busy areas next to guests, shops or homes. A factory-made frame means less cutting, mixing and dust on site. ILO states site waste of about 2% for light gauge steel, compared with about 20% for conventional methods. Our guide to building on a tight plot covers access and neighbours in more detail.

What it costs

The published ILO price tiers are for villas. Frame and roof starts from about Rp 2.5 million per square metre, a weather-tight lockup shell is about Rp 4 million per square metre, and a turnkey villa typically runs Rp 7 to 11 million per square metre. Commercial buildings vary more in use, loads and finish, so we do not publish a single rate for them. Send your drawings or brief and we will quote the scope. Some owners want ILO to build the whole project. Others have a contractor and only need the structure. For a builder who wants the frame package, Infinity Structural frame kits are supplied or supplied and erected in Bali and Lombok. What a package includes is explained in what a light gauge steel package includes.

Questions to ask any builder about a commercial building

  • Has the builder delivered commercial or hospitality buildings, not only villas?
  • Who designs the structure, and who checks spans and floor loads?
  • How many days will the frame take on site, and what happens before and after?
  • What is included in the price: frame, roof, cladding, fit-out?
  • Which fire and safety rules apply to my use, and who handles them?

Frequently asked questions

Can light gauge steel be used for commercial buildings in Bali?

Yes. ILO builds commercial buildings as well as villas and townhouses, including warehouses, offices, retail spaces and hospitality buildings. Each building is engineered for its own loads and use.

Can steel frame handle open floor plans and large spans?

ILO describes large-span capability for commercial buildings. How far a frame can span without support depends on the design, so your engineer confirms spans and loads for each project.

How tall can a light gauge steel building be?

ILO states that LGS can support mid-rise structures up to 10 storeys. Most Bali projects are 2 to 3 storeys, and every multi-storey job is engineered separately.

Does a steel frame help a café or hotel open sooner?

A faster build can mean earlier opening and earlier income. Projects are typically completed 2 to 3 times faster than traditional methods. The full programme depends on size, finish level and site.
