Two-Storey Steel Frame Villas in Bali

What changes when a steel frame villa has an upper floor.

Many Bali buyers want a second floor for the view, the extra bedrooms or a rooftop living space. A light gauge steel (LGS) frame can carry a second storey. The design just needs more care than a single-storey villa. This guide explains how the upper floor works, what to settle before you ask for prices, and what to ask a builder. It does not replace your engineer, who sets the final design for your plot.

Yes, a steel frame can have two storeys

Two-storey villas are part of the project scope ILO discusses. You can see a two-storey LGS villa frame in our photo galleries. The key point is that each two-storey job is engineered on its own. The frame, the floor interfaces and any hybrid steel need project-specific engineering and a quotation. A standard single-storey price does not carry over.

How the upper floor is built

An LGS floor is made of C-section joists connected to C-section bearers. The joists can be designed from various C-section sizes, depending on the loads they must carry. In plain terms, the floor is a steel grid that spreads the weight of people, furniture and walls and limits how much the floor bends.

ILO makes floor frames in its factory in Banyuwangi, Java. One of the gallery photos shows prefabricated floor cassettes on the factory floor. Making the floor in a factory keeps the parts accurate before they travel to site. For more detail on walls, floors and roofs, read what is light gauge steel.

Where the pieces meet

On a two-storey villa, the weak points are the joins. The upper floor must connect to the walls below. The walls above must connect to the floor. The roof must tie to the top walls. The whole building must tie to its foundation. A light frame only performs well if these links are designed and built correctly. Our guide to steel frames in Bali's earthquake zone explains why connections matter so much.

The foundation also matters. A second storey changes the loads that reach the ground, so your engineer must check it. Read foundations for a steel frame villa for the basics.

Floors, toppings and stairs: check the scope

Upper-floor decking, toppings and stairs are not automatically included in a two-storey kit. These are the items that cause most arguments about price, so agree them early. Foundations and slabs, wall and roof coverings, services, permits and professional design are also outside a frame kit unless expressly agreed. What it costs

ILO prices by build stage. Frame and roof starts from about Rp 2.5 million per square metre, a weather-tight lockup shell is about Rp 4 million, and a turnkey villa typically runs Rp 7 to 11 million per square metre depending on finish. For frame kits, the single-storey planning rate does not apply to a two-storey villa. Two-storey packages are quoted per project. Our cost guide explains each stage in plain words.

Questions to ask any builder about a second storey

  • Who engineers the upper floor, and will I see the drawings before manufacture?
  • Are decking, floor toppings and stairs in the price?
  • How is the upper floor fixed to the walls below?
  • Has the foundation been checked for the extra load?
  • Who is responsible for the joins between the frame and other trades?

Frequently asked questions

Can you build a two-storey villa with a steel frame in Bali?

Yes. Two-storey villas are part of the project scope ILO discusses. The frame, the floor interfaces and any hybrid steel need project-specific engineering and a quotation.

Is a two-storey villa priced the same as a single-storey one?

No. The single-storey frame kit planning rate does not apply to a two-storey villa. Two-storey packages are quoted per project after engineering.

Are upper-floor decking and stairs included in the frame kit?

Not automatically. Upper-floor decking, toppings and stairs are not part of a two-storey kit unless agreed. Settle these interfaces with your engineer and builder before you compare quotes.

How is the upper floor built in light gauge steel?

The floor is a set of C-section joists connected to C-section bearers. The joists are sized to the loads they must carry, and ILO makes floor frames in its factory.

Why do two-storey villas need their own engineering?

The upper floor adds load paths, connections and stair openings that a single-storey plan does not have. Each design needs its own check before manufacture.
