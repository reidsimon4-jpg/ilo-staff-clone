Steel frames for multi-villa developments in Bali

How repeat plans, factory frames and shorter programmes help developers.

A single villa is a project. A row of villas, a set of townhouses or a small resort is a programme. The way you build changes when the same plan repeats several times. This guide explains how light gauge steel (LGS) framing suits multi-unit developments in Bali, and what developers should check before they commit.

Why repeat units suit a steel frame

LGS frames are made in a factory from a drawing. Wall panels, floor frames and roof trusses are cut and formed by machine. ILO makes them at its own factory in Banyuwangi, Java, using computer-controlled roll-forming.

When ten units share one plan, the factory repeats the same parts. The setup is done once. Fit problems are found on the first unit and fixed for all the rest. That is hard to match with block work, where each wall is built by hand on site.

What types of development can steel frame handle?

ILO builds luxury residential villas, multi-family townhouses, resort accommodation and commercial buildings. Light gauge steel can support mid-rise buildings up to 10 storeys. The two and three storey buildings common in Bali sit comfortably inside that range.

Programme: work on two fronts at once

On a concrete build, most jobs follow each other. The frame cannot start until the foundations are cured, and the walls take months. With steel, the frames are made in Java while the site team prepares the ground and foundations. The two jobs overlap.

LGS projects are completed two to three times faster than traditional methods. A standard villa superstructure that takes months in concrete can be erected in days. For a developer, a shorter programme means lower financing costs and earlier rental income. See the full sequence in From factory to keys: the ILO build timeline.

Tight sites and neighbours

Many Bali plots sit in dense areas such as Canggu, Seminyak and Ubud. Prefabricated frames arrive ready to assemble, so there is less cutting, mixing and waste on site. This keeps site disruption lower in dense tourist areas. Ask any builder how they will handle deliveries, storage and access before you sign.

Earthquakes and tropical conditions

A steel frame is much lighter than concrete, so it attracts lower earthquake forces. Steel is ductile, which means it flexes and absorbs energy rather than failing suddenly. Galvanised steel gives termites nothing to eat and cannot rot or grow mould. Read more in steel frame vs concrete in Bali's earthquake zone and termites, humidity and rust.

Good design is still required. The frame must be engineered for each building, and coverings and details must be installed correctly.

What does it cost across several units?

ILO prices by build stage. An engineered frame and roof package starts from about Rp 2.5 million per square metre. A weather-tight lockup shell is about Rp 4 million per square metre. Questions to ask before you commit

  • Is the price for frame only, lockup or turnkey?
  • Are permits and design included, or separate?
  • Who makes the frames, and can you visit the factory?
  • How will deliveries reach a narrow or busy site?
  • Who erects the frames, and who is responsible for fit?

Frequently asked questions

Is light gauge steel suitable for a villa complex or resort in Bali?

Yes. ILO builds villas, multi-family townhouses, resort accommodation and commercial buildings with light gauge steel. Repeating the same frame across several units suits the method well.

Does repeating one villa design save time and money?

It usually helps. Identical wall panels, floor frames and roof trusses are set up once in the factory and then repeated. Less redesign means fewer errors and a steadier programme. Savings depend on the project, so ask for a quote on your actual plan.

Can the foundations be built while the frames are being made?

Yes. The frames are made at the ILO factory in Banyuwangi, Java, while the site team prepares the ground and foundations. The two jobs overlap, which is one reason steel frame builds finish sooner.

How tall can a light gauge steel building be?

Light gauge steel can support mid-rise buildings up to 10 storeys. Most Bali villas and townhouses are two or three storeys, which is well within that range.

Can a builder buy just the frames for a development?

Yes. Infinity Structural supplies frame kits to builders and developers in Bali and Lombok. The kit covers the agreed structural components only, so foundations, coverings, finishes and services need their own allowances.
