LGS vs. Traditional Block Work in Bali

Why developers are choosing Light Gauge Steel over conventional concrete blocks.

Traditional block construction in Bali has long been the norm, but it comes with several drawbacks: high material costs, long build times, and vulnerability to seismic activity. Light Gauge Steel (LGS) offers a modern alternative that addresses these issues.

Cost Efficiency

LGS components are fabricated off‑site, reducing on‑site labor and waste. This results in up to 30% cost savings compared to block work.

Speed of Construction

Modular LGS frames can be erected in days rather than weeks, accelerating project timelines and reducing financing costs.

Structural Benefits

Steel frames provide superior strength‑to‑weight ratios and excellent seismic performance, crucial for Bali’s earthquake‑prone regions.
