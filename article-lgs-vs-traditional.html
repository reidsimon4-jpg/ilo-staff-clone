LGS vs. Traditional Block Work in Bali

Why developers are choosing Light Gauge Steel over conventional concrete blocks.

Block and concrete construction has long been the default in Bali. It works, but it is slow, it creates a lot of waste, and a heavy building attracts large forces in an earthquake. Light gauge steel (LGS) is a different way to build the frame. This article compares the two on the points that matter to a villa owner, investor or developer.

If you are new to steel framing, start with What is LGS? and then come back here.

What is the difference in simple terms?

In block work, the walls are built on site from blocks, mortar and reinforced concrete columns and beams. Most of the work happens outdoors, in the weather, one layer at a time.

In LGS, the frame is made from thin galvanised steel sections that are rolled into C-shapes. ILO makes them in its own factory in Banyuwangi, Java. The frame arrives as wall panels, roof trusses and floor sections. Crews then assemble it on site. LGS frames are rolled cold, to ASTM standards, with a yield strength of 230-340 MPa.

Cost: what the numbers say

Total project cost with LGS is generally 25-40% lower than traditional concrete and brick, once speed and waste savings are included. The saving does not come from cheaper finishes. It comes from less labour, a shorter build, lighter foundations and almost no wasted material.

Pricing is by build stage:

  • An engineered frame and roof package starts at about Rp 2.5 million per square metre.
  • A weather-tight lockup shell is about Rp 4 million per square metre.
  • A complete turnkey villa is typically Rp 7-11 million per square metre, depending on finish level and location.

Speed: days instead of months

LGS projects are typically completed 2 to 3 times faster than traditional methods. Because the frame is made off site with millimetre precision, a villa superstructure that takes months in concrete can be erected in days. Foundation work and site preparation can happen while the frame is being made.

For an investor, a shorter build means less time paying interest and an earlier start to rental income. See our process for the steps from design to handover.

Earthquake performance

Bali sits on the Pacific Ring of Fire. Earthquake forces on a building rise with its weight. Steel framing is far lighter than concrete, so it attracts much lower seismic forces. Steel is also ductile. It bends and absorbs energy instead of cracking suddenly. That gives people more time to get out.

Every building still needs proper engineering for its site. A steel frame helps, but it does not replace good design and good connections.

Termites, moisture and fire

Steel is inorganic. Termites cannot eat it, and it does not rot, warp or grow mould in humid weather. That also removes the need for chemical soil treatment that timber needs in the tropics. The zinc or aluminium-zinc coating protects the steel from rust, and a properly installed and enclosed frame has a structural life of more than 50 years.

Steel is also non-combustible. It does not burn or add fuel to a fire. The benefits page covers these points in more detail.

Waste and sustainability

Site waste is about 2% with LGS, compared with about 20% for conventional methods. Steel contains at least 25% recycled content and is 100% recyclable. Because the frame is cut to length in the factory, less material is wasted on your block.

Where block work still makes sense

Block and concrete are familiar to every local crew, and they suit retaining walls, below-ground structures and some ground slabs. LGS villas still sit on a foundation, and lighter frames allow lighter foundations. The two methods work together. The question is which one should form the frame of the building.

Frequently asked questions

Is light gauge steel cheaper than block work in Bali?

Total project cost is generally 25-40% lower than concrete and brick once speed and waste savings are counted. Published ILO prices start at about Rp 2.5 million per square metre for an engineered frame and roof package, and a turnkey villa runs about Rp 7-11 million per square metre depending on finish level and location.

How much faster is a steel frame than block work?

Projects are typically completed 2 to 3 times faster. The frame is made in ILO's Java factory and assembled on site, so a villa superstructure that takes months in concrete can be erected in days.

Is a steel frame safe in an earthquake?

Steel framing is much lighter than concrete, so it attracts lower seismic forces. The steel is also ductile, which means it flexes and absorbs energy instead of failing suddenly. Bali sits on the Pacific Ring of Fire, so this matters.

Do termites or humidity damage a steel frame?

No. Steel is not a food source for termites and it does not rot or grow mould. The zinc or aluminium-zinc coating protects it from rust when the frame is properly installed and enclosed.

Can I still use block, render or stone finishes?

Yes. The steel frame is the structure. Cladding, render, stone and interior finishes can be added on top, so the look of the villa is a design choice and not a limit of the frame.
