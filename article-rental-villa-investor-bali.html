Building a rental villa in Bali with steel frame

What investors should check on speed, cost stages and risk.

Many Bali villas are built to earn rent, not just to live in. For an investor, the build method changes how soon the villa opens, how much waste you pay for, and how clear the price is. This guide explains where light gauge steel (LGS) framing helps a rental villa, and what to check before you commit. It does not promise any rental income, because that depends on location, management and the market.

Why build time matters for a rental villa

A villa earns nothing until it opens. Every month of construction is a month of financing costs, land costs and lost bookings. LGS projects are completed two to three times faster than traditional methods. A standard villa superstructure that takes months in concrete can be erected in days.

The speed comes from the factory. ILO makes the wall panels, floor frames and roof trusses at its own factory in Banyuwangi, Java, while the site team prepares the ground and foundations. The two jobs overlap. See the full sequence in From factory to keys: the ILO build timeline.

Cost: look at the whole project

Do not compare only the price of the frame. Compare the total project. Total project costs with LGS are generally 25 to 40 percent lower than traditional concrete and brick once speed and waste savings are included. Part of that is waste. Steel frames are cut by machine to a drawing, so material waste is about 2 percent, against about 20 percent for traditional methods.

These are general figures. Your own saving depends on the design, the finish level and the site. Ask for a quote on your actual plan.

Understand the price stages

ILO prices by build stage, so you can see what you are buying:

  • Frame and roof: an engineered frame and roof package starts from about Rp 2.5 million per square metre.
  • Lockup shell: a weather-tight shell is about Rp 4 million per square metre.
  • Turnkey: a complete villa typically runs Rp 7 to 11 million per square metre, depending on finish level and location.

For an investor, turnkey is usually the number that matters, because it is the stage where the villa can open. Our cost guide explains what each stage includes, and the Build & Price tool gives a quick estimate.

Durability in the tropics

A rental villa works hard. It is used often, in heat and humidity, and repairs cost both money and bookings. Galvanised steel is termite-proof, rot-proof and cannot grow mould. When properly installed and enclosed, LGS frames have a structural lifespan beyond 50 years, even in the tropical climate. Read more in termites, humidity and rust.

Steel is also non-combustible. It does not ignite or add fuel to a fire, which can reduce insurance premiums for property owners. Check this with your own insurer.

Earthquake safety for guests and owners

Bali sits in an earthquake zone. A steel frame is much lighter than concrete, so it attracts lower earthquake forces. Steel is also ductile, so it flexes and absorbs energy rather than failing suddenly. Good engineering and correct installation are still essential. Our guide to steel frame vs concrete in Bali's earthquake zone explains the reasoning.

Risks and what to check

  • Is the quote frame only, lockup or turnkey?
  • Are design, permits, foundations and services included or separate?
  • Who makes the frames, and can you visit the factory?
  • Who erects the frames and is responsible for fit?
  • What is excluded from the price?

Frequently asked questions

Does a steel frame villa start earning rent sooner?

It can. Light gauge steel projects are completed two to three times faster than traditional methods, and a standard villa superstructure that takes months in concrete can be erected in days. A shorter programme means the villa can open earlier. The total timeline still depends on design, permits, foundations and finishes.

Is steel frame cheaper than concrete for a Bali villa?

Total project costs are generally 25 to 40 percent lower than traditional concrete and brick once speed and waste savings are included. Material waste is about 2 percent with steel, compared with 20 percent for traditional methods. Your actual saving depends on the design and finish level, so ask for a quote on your plan.

How much does a turnkey steel frame villa cost per square metre?

A complete turnkey villa typically runs Rp 7 to 11 million per square metre, depending on finish level and location. An engineered frame and roof package starts from about Rp 2.5 million per square metre, and a weather-tight lockup shell is about Rp 4 million per square metre.

Will termites and humidity shorten the life of a rental villa?

Galvanised steel gives termites nothing to eat and cannot rot or grow mould. When properly installed and enclosed, LGS frames have a structural lifespan beyond 50 years, even in the tropical climate. Coverings and details still need correct installation and normal upkeep.

What should an investor ask a builder before signing?

Ask whether the price covers frame only, lockup or turnkey, whether permits and design are included, who makes and erects the frames, and what is excluded. Our eight questions for choosing a villa builder go through each point.
