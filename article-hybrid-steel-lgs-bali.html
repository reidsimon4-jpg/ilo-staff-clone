Hybrid Steel: LGS with Heavier Beams in Bali

When a light gauge steel villa needs a few heavier steel members, and how that is engineered and quoted.

Most of a steel frame villa is made from light gauge steel (LGS). These are thin, cold-formed C-shaped studs, tracks, joists and trusses. They suit walls, floors and roofs very well. But some designs also need a few heavier steel members. When that happens, the result is called a hybrid structure. This article explains what that means, in plain words, for villa owners, builders and developers in Bali and Lombok.

What hybrid steel means

ILO states it simply: LGS can be integrated with WF, SHS or RHS steel members where the project design requires it. The three letters stand for:

  • WF (wide flange): the familiar I-shaped beam.
  • SHS (square hollow section): a square steel tube.
  • RHS (rectangular hollow section): a rectangular steel tube.

These members are much heavier than an LGS stud. They are used in the same way heavy steel is used in any building: where a single member has to carry more load than a light section is designed for. The LGS frame handles the walls, floors and roof around them.

Why a design might need it

Whether a villa needs hybrid steel is an engineering decision, not a sales choice. It depends on the drawings. Features that often lead an engineer to look at heavier members include wide openings, large roof or floor spans, and places where loads are concentrated. If your architect has drawn a large glazed opening, an open-plan living area, or a roof terrace, ask early whether the frame can handle it with LGS alone.

The reverse is also true. Many villas need no hybrid steel at all. A standard villa layout is well suited to LGS walls, floors and trusses. For the basics, read what is light gauge steel.

What stays the same

A hybrid frame is still made in ILO's own factory in Banyuwangi, Java. The LGS parts are roll formed to the approved design, as they would be for any kit. Factory production is not slowed by rain. How the heavier members are supplied is agreed in the project scope, along with their connections.

Where the risk sits: the connections

Whenever two different systems meet, the connection is the part to get right. A light stud wall must bear on or fix to a heavy beam in a way the engineer has checked. Because of this, ILO treats hybrid connections and members as separately engineered and quoted items. They are not part of the standard kit. How hybrid is priced

Published prices cover standard scopes. For frame kits, a single-storey kit is about Rp 660,000 per square metre of floor area ex-factory, based on an 11 kg/m² planning assumption. That is an indicative reference only. It does not price two-storey, installed or hybrid packages. Hybrid work is quoted project by project after engineering.

For villas priced by build stage, frame and roof starts from about Rp 2.5 million per square metre, a lockup shell is about Rp 4 million, and a turnkey villa typically runs Rp 7-11 million per square metre, depending on finish and location. See the villa cost guide for what each stage includes.

What else sits outside the kit

Foundations and slabs, wall and roof coverings, services, permits and professional design are outside a frame kit unless expressly agreed. Freight, ferry, unloading, cranes and erection need a defined allowance. Heavier members can change the lifting needs on site, so raise access and crane questions at the start. The frame kit package guide lists the full scope.

What to send for a hybrid enquiry

  • Architectural drawings or a concept plan.
  • Project location, total floor area and storey count.
  • The openings, spans or roof features you think may need heavier steel.
  • Whether you want supply only or supply and erect.
  • Site access details, including roads, unloading and lifting space.

Frequently asked questions

What does hybrid steel mean in a villa frame?

It means a light gauge steel (LGS) frame combined with heavier steel members such as WF, SHS or RHS sections where the design needs them. Hybrid connections and members are separately engineered and quoted.

What are WF, SHS and RHS steel?

WF means wide flange, the I-shaped beam. SHS is a square hollow section and RHS is a rectangular hollow section. All three are heavier structural steel members than the thin C-sections used in an LGS frame.

Is hybrid steel included in the frame kit price?

No. The single-storey kit planning rate of about Rp 660,000 per square metre does not price hybrid structures. Hybrid connections and members are engineered and quoted for each project.

Who decides whether my villa needs hybrid steel?

The design does, checked by the project engineer. Share your drawings early. ILO then defines the structural boundary and interfaces with the appointed project professionals before manufacture.

Does hybrid steel make the villa slower to build?

The LGS frame is still made in the factory, so that work does not wait on weather. The extra step is design and coordination, so settle the hybrid parts before production release rather than on site.
