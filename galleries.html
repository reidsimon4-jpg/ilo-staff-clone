Common Questions About the Galleries

New to steel framing? Start with what LGS is, then see LGS construction in Bali.

What do the ILO galleries show?

The galleries follow a light gauge steel (LGS) building from start to finish. You can see steel frames and roof trusses in the factory, floor cassettes ready to ship, wall panels and floors going up on site in Bali, and finished villas, townhouses and commercial spaces.

Where are the frames in the photos made?

The frames are made in ILO's own factory in Banyuwangi, East Java. The galleries include photos of the factory hall, with panel racks and a delivery truck, and of floor cassettes ready to ship. See the factory page for more.

How does the frame go up on site?

The frame arrives as ready-made wall panels, floor cassettes and roof trusses. A spider crane places the floor cassettes and stands the wall panels, so the structure can go up on site in days. The build process page lists the steps.

Can an LGS villa have a normal finished look?

Yes. The steel frame is the structure only. The photos show GRC wall panels installed on LGS frames, and villa interiors with glass doors and a pool. Cladding, render and stone can also be added on top of the frame.
