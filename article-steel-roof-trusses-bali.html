Steel Roof Trusses for Bali Villas

Roof shapes, coverings and fixings for a light gauge steel villa.

The roof is the part of a villa most people notice first. It also has to hold up against Bali's wind and rain, year after year. In a light gauge steel (LGS) villa, the roof frame is made of steel trusses instead of timber. This guide explains what that means, which roof shapes work, and what to ask a builder. It does not replace your engineer, who sets the final roof design for your plot.

What is a steel roof truss?

A truss is a ready-made roof frame. Steel coil is rolled into C-sections without heat, and those sections are joined into triangles that span the walls. The triangles carry the roof covering and pass the load down into the wall frame. ILO describes its roof system as a steel truss system designed for metal sheets or tiles. For a plain-English view of the whole frame, see what is light gauge steel.

Roof shapes that work

A steel truss system can accommodate the common roof designs: hip, gable and dutch gable.

  • Gable: two sloping sides that meet at a ridge, with a flat wall at each end.
  • Hip: all four sides slope down to the walls.
  • Dutch gable: a hip roof with a small gable on top, often used for a more detailed look.

You do not have to give up a hip, gable or dutch gable look to use a steel frame. Share your drawings early so the trusses can be set out to suit.

Metal sheet or tile

The truss system is generally designed for metal sheets or tiles. Both are common in Bali. The covering matters because it changes the weight the truss must carry. Tell your builder which covering you plan to use before the roof frame is designed, not after. Coverings are not part of a frame kit price unless expressly agreed, so check who supplies them.

How the roof is fixed to the walls

The trusses are screwed directly onto the wall frame. Because the wall panels and the trusses are made in the same factory to the same drawings, the parts are designed to meet. A roof is only as good as its connections. The walls, floors and roof must be tied together, and the whole building must be tied to its foundation. A weak link at any point undoes the benefit of a light frame. Our guide to steel frame vs concrete in Bali's earthquake zone explains why this matters.

ILO also lists two framing options for roofs: custom stick-built rafters, or pre-engineered truss assemblies that span the exterior walls. Your engineer and builder choose between them for each design.

Why steel suits a tropical roof

Timber roof frames face termites, damp and rot. Galvanized steel is termite-proof and rot-proof, so the roof frame needs no termite treatment. The steel is also non-combustible. The roof covering, flashings and waterproofing still need normal upkeep, as on any villa. For more on this, read how steel frames hold up in the tropics.

Made in the factory, then lifted into place

ILO makes wall panels, floor frames and roof trusses at its factory in Banyuwangi, Java, while the site team prepares the foundations. When the parts arrive, the crew can start assembling. See the full sequence in From factory to keys: the ILO build timeline.

Questions to ask any builder about the roof

  • Who designs the roof trusses, and are the drawings shared before manufacture?
  • Is the roof covering included in the price, or separate?
  • How are the trusses fixed to the wall frame?
  • Is the steel galvanized, and what is the coating?
  • Who is responsible for waterproofing and flashings?

Frequently asked questions

Can steel trusses be used for a hip, gable or dutch gable roof?

Yes. A steel truss system can accommodate hip, gable and dutch gable roof designs. The trusses are screwed directly onto the wall frame.

Can a steel truss roof take metal sheets or tiles?

Yes. A steel truss system is generally designed for metal sheets or tiles. Tell your builder which covering you want early, because the covering affects the truss design.

Are roof trusses included in a frame kit?

Yes. The Infinity Structural kit includes structural panels and trusses made to the approved design. Wall and roof coverings are outside the kit unless expressly agreed.

Do steel roof trusses suffer from termites?

No. Galvanized steel is termite-proof and rot-proof, so the truss frame needs no termite treatment. Roofing and waterproofing still need normal upkeep, as on any villa.

Where are the trusses made?

At the ILO factory in Banyuwangi, Java. They are made while the site team prepares the ground and foundations, so the two jobs overlap.
