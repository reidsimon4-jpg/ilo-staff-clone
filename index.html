Infinity Living Options — LGS Construction Company Indonesia

Infinity Living Options (ILO) is Indonesia's leading prefab modular light gauge steel (LGS) construction company, headquartered in Bali with a manufacturing factory in Java. ILO builds villas, townhouses, and commercial buildings using cold-formed galvanized steel frames that are 2-3x faster to construct than traditional brick and concrete, cost 25-40% less, and produce 90% less site waste. All steel is ASTM-certified (230-340 MPa yield strength), 100% recyclable, earthquake-resistant, termite-proof, and backed by a 50+ year structural warranty. Questions About LGS Construction

LGS construction uses cold-formed galvanized steel profiles to create structural frames for buildings. It is a modern, modular alternative to timber and concrete, offering superior strength, speed, and sustainability — ideal for Indonesia's climate and seismic conditions.

LGS modular construction is typically 2-3x faster than conventional methods. Our prefabricated frames are manufactured in our Java factory and assembled on-site in days rather than weeks, dramatically reducing your project timeline.

Yes. LGS frames are lightweight and flexible, providing excellent seismic performance. The steel's ductility allows the structure to absorb and dissipate earthquake energy, making it ideal for Indonesia's seismically active regions.

LGS construction typically costs 25-40% less than conventional methods when you factor in speed savings, reduced waste, and lower labour costs. Use our cost calculator above for an instant estimate, or contact us for a detailed project quote.

Absolutely. Galvanized steel is immune to corrosion, termites, rot, and mould — the major challenges of building in Indonesia's humid tropical environment. LGS frames are backed by 50+ year structural warranties.
