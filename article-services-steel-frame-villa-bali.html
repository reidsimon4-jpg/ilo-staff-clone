Wiring, Plumbing and Air-Con in a Steel Frame Villa

How power, water and cooling run through a light gauge steel frame.

Buyers often ask how electrical, plumbing and air-conditioning work inside a steel frame villa. There is no brick to chase and no concrete to cut. The answer is simpler than many expect, because the frame is made with services in mind. This guide explains how it works, who does what, and what to ask before you sign. Your electrician, plumber and engineer set the detail for your villa.

Where services go in a steel frame

A light gauge steel (LGS) wall is a row of steel studs with a gap between them. That gap is called the wall cavity. Cables and pipes run inside it. The finished wall lining covers them. If you are new to the system, start with what is light gauge steel.

Pre-punched holes

ILO makes its frames in its own factory in Banyuwangi, Java. The steel sections have pre-punched holes for electrical and plumbing runs. That means installers do not need to cut in or drill on site. Factory-provided holes remove on-site drilling, and the frame keeps its shape, so pipes stay fixed inside stable wall cavities.

Electrical work

Cables pass through the punched holes along the wall. Steel does not burn, which helps fire safety. If there is an electrical fault, the conductive steel frame trips the circuit breaker quickly. This is one reason all wiring must be done by a licensed electrician to the right standard, with correct earthing. ILO does not replace that professional advice.

Plumbing

Water supply and waste pipes use the same pre-punched holes. Because the walls are straight and the frame does not warp, pipes sit securely in the cavity. Wet areas such as bathrooms still need proper waterproofing, which is part of the finishing stage and not the steel frame itself.

Air-conditioning

Most Bali villas rely on air-conditioning. Pre-punched service holes help route ducts and pipework. Straight walls and square corners give predictable pathways, which makes pre-assembly easier. Your designer still plans where indoor units, outdoor units and condensate drains go. Raise this early so the right holes and supports are planned before the frame is made.

Sound and insulation

A common question is whether steel frame houses are noisy inside. The frame is not the cause. Sound performance depends on designing the wall with proper insulation in the cavity. Cavity insulation also improves sound control. If quiet matters, for example between bedrooms or for a rental villa, ask what insulation is allowed for in your quote.

Who installs what, and when

Finishing covers external cladding, internal linings, electrical, plumbing and air-conditioning services, and final finishes. It ends with a quality inspection and handover with full documentation. The frame goes up first, services follow inside the walls, then the linings close them in. You can see the full sequence in From factory to keys: the ILO build timeline.

Questions to ask any builder about services

  • Are electrical, plumbing and air-conditioning in the price, or separate?
  • Who is licensed to do the electrical work?
  • Is wall insulation included, and which walls get it?
  • When do you need my air-conditioning and bathroom plans?
  • How are wet areas waterproofed?

Frequently asked questions

Can electrical and plumbing be installed in a steel frame wall?

Yes. The steel sections have pre-punched holes for electrical and plumbing runs, so installers do not need to cut in or drill on site. Pipes and cables run inside the wall cavities.

Does a steel frame make air-conditioning harder to fit?

No. Pre-punched service holes help route ducts, and straight walls and square corners give predictable pathways. Your air-conditioning designer still plans unit positions and drainage.

Is a steel frame villa noisier inside?

Not because of the steel. Sound performance depends on designing the wall with proper insulation in the cavity, not on the frame material. Ask what insulation is allowed for in your quote.

Is a steel frame safer in an electrical fault?

Steel does not burn. If there is an electrical fault, the conductive steel frame trips the circuit breaker quickly. All wiring must still be done by a licensed electrician to the correct standard.

Are services included in a frame kit price?

No. A frame kit covers the steel frame. Cladding, linings, electrical, plumbing and air-conditioning belong to the finishing stage, which is included in a turnkey villa price but not in frame-and-roof or lockup pricing.
