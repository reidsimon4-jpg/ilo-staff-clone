The LGS Construction Process

From initial consultation to project completion — our streamlined modular construction process delivers LGS projects across Indonesia up to 3x faster than conventional methods.

Step by Step

From Concept to Completion

Our proven 8-step modular LGS construction process ensures quality, speed, and transparency at every stage.

01

Consultation & Planning

Initial consultation to understand your project requirements, site conditions, budget, and design preferences. We assess feasibility for modular LGS construction and outline the project scope for your Indonesia build.

02

Architectural & Structural Design

Our engineering team creates detailed CAD-based architectural and structural designs optimised for light gauge steel framing. Designs account for Indonesia's seismic requirements, tropical climate, and your aesthetic preferences.

03

Permits & Regulatory Compliance

We navigate Indonesia's building permits and regulatory requirements using our extensive knowledge of local compliance. Our team handles the complexities so you can focus on your project vision.

04

Factory Manufacturing

Precision manufacturing of LGS components in our Java factory. Galvanized steel coils are cold-formed into C-sections, tracks, studs, and custom profiles — cut to exact specifications with computer-controlled accuracy.

05

Frame Prefabrication

Individual components are assembled into complete wall frames, floor systems, and roof trusses within our controlled factory environment. This improves quality, reduces on-site errors, and dramatically accelerates construction timelines.

06

Transport & Logistics

Efficient packaging and shipment of prefabricated LGS frames from our Banyuwangi factory to your construction site — whether in Bali, Java, or elsewhere across Indonesia's archipelago.

07

On-Site Assembly

Rapid assembly of prefabricated steel frames on-site. Our experienced crews erect wall panels, floor systems, and roof trusses with speed and precision — significantly faster than conventional construction methods in Indonesia.

08

Finishing & Handover

Integration of external cladding, internal linings, MEP services (electrical, plumbing, HVAC), and final finishes. Thorough quality inspection and project handover with full documentation.
