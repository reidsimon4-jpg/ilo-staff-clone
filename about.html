Our Story

Pioneering LGS Construction in Indonesia

Infinity Living Options was founded with a clear vision: to bring advanced, sustainable construction solutions to Indonesia's rapidly evolving real estate market. Recognising the increasing demand for durable, eco-friendly, and efficient building methods, we set out to introduce innovative light gauge steel technology to villa and townhouse development across Bali and Indonesia.

Our goal is to help developers, architects, and homeowners streamline their projects, reduce construction time, and minimise environmental impact without compromising quality or design. By integrating modern engineering practices with Bali's unique architectural landscape, we provide tailored modular construction solutions that enhance both functionality and aesthetics.

What does Infinity Living Options do?

Infinity Living Options (ILO) is a prefab light gauge steel (LGS) builder. It designs and builds villas, townhouses and commercial buildings in Bali and across Indonesia. It also supplies steel frame kits to builders and developers through Infinity Structural.

Where does ILO make its steel frames?

ILO has its own factory in Banyuwangi, East Java. The frames are roll formed there, packed and shipped to site, whether in Bali, Java or elsewhere in Indonesia.

Who does ILO work with?

ILO works with villa owners, investors, developers, architects and builders. Villa owners and investors can start at the contact page. Builders and developers who want frame kits only can use the Infinity Structural page.

Where does ILO build?

ILO's service area is Indonesia-wide, including Bali, Lombok, Jakarta and Java. Its Bali page covers Canggu, Ubud, Seminyak, Uluwatu and Sanur.
