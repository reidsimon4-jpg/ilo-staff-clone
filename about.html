Our Story

Pioneering LGS Construction in Indonesia

Infinity Living Options was founded with a clear vision: to bring advanced, sustainable construction solutions to Indonesia's rapidly evolving real estate market. Recognising the increasing demand for durable, eco-friendly, and efficient building methods, we set out to introduce innovative light gauge steel technology to villa and townhouse development across Bali and Indonesia.

Our goal is to help developers, architects, and homeowners streamline their projects, reduce construction time, and minimise environmental impact without compromising quality or design. By integrating modern engineering practices with Bali's unique architectural landscape, we provide tailored modular construction solutions that enhance both functionality and aesthetics.
