Building a Villa in Lombok with a Steel Frame

Factory-made frames, delivered to your plot.

Lombok is the next island east of Bali, and more villa owners and developers are looking at it. Building there raises a simple question: how do you get a quality building to a site that may be far from a city and its suppliers? Light gauge steel (LGS) is a good fit for that problem, because most of the work is done in a factory and not on the plot. This guide explains how a steel frame villa is built in Lombok, in plain English.

Why Lombok suits a factory-made frame

On a remote plot, every delivery of sand, cement, blocks and timber has to travel a long way. Block work also needs a large site team for many months. A factory-made frame changes this. The structure is made under cover at ILO's own factory in Banyuwangi, East Java, so weather does not slow it. The frames then arrive ready to assemble. If you are new to the system, read what is light gauge steel first.

How the frame gets to Lombok

The steel starts as coils and is roll formed into studs, tracks, joists and trusses in the factory. Finished wall panels, floors and trusses are packed and shipped from Banyuwangi to the site. ILO delivers to Bali, Lombok, Jakarta and elsewhere in Indonesia. For Lombok, the route includes freight and ferry. Freight, ferry, unloading, cranes and erection each need their own allowance in the budget, so ask for them to be listed. Our guide to what a frame kit package includes explains what is in the kit and what is not.

Check the site before you order

A factory-made frame needs a plan for the last part of the trip. Before you order, check these points with your builder:

  • Can a truck reach the plot, and is the access road wide enough?
  • Is there room to unload and store the panels, and is there a place for a crane or lifting gear?
  • Where will the panels be assembled, and who will do it?
  • Are power and water available on site during the build?

Roads, unloading, lifting and assembly space all affect the delivery and erection plan. It is better to find these out early than on the day the frames arrive.

Foundations and the build sequence

A lighter frame allows lighter foundations, but the foundation is still designed by your engineer for each site. The footings and the slab are built on site while the frame is made in the factory. This is how the two jobs run side by side and save time. For more detail, see our guide to foundations for a steel frame villa, and the ILO build timeline for the order of work. We do not quote fixed weeks for each step, because they depend on the site and the design.

What it costs

Published ILO prices are guides, and they depend on finish level and location. An engineered frame-and-roof package starts at about Rp 2.5 million per square metre. A weather-tight lockup shell is about Rp 4 million per square metre. A complete turnkey villa is typically Rp 7-11 million per square metre. Remote sites can add transport and site costs, so ask for a quote for your plot. The villa cost guide explains what each tier includes, and the Build & Price tool gives an instant estimate.

Built to last in the tropics

Lombok is hot and humid, like Bali. The steel carries a zinc or aluminium-zinc coating. Steel is not food for termites, and it does not rot or grow mould. A frame that is properly installed and enclosed has a structural life of more than 50 years. Our article on termites, humidity and rust covers this in more detail. For earthquake design, read steel frame vs concrete in an earthquake zone.

Questions to ask your builder

  • Who handles freight and the ferry crossing, and is it in the quote?
  • Who assembles the frame on site, and who supervises?
  • What access and lifting equipment does the plot need?
  • Which price tier is quoted, and what is left out?
  • What does the engineer need to know about the soil and the site?

Frequently asked questions

Does ILO build in Lombok?

Yes. ILO builds across Bali, Lombok and Jakarta, and delivers frames elsewhere in Indonesia. The frames are made in the factory in Banyuwangi, East Java, and shipped to site.

How does the steel frame get to Lombok?

Finished wall panels, floors and trusses are packed at the factory and transported to site. For Lombok this includes freight and a ferry crossing. These costs need their own allowance, so ask for them to be listed in your quote.

Is building in Lombok more expensive than in Bali?

Published prices depend on finish level and location. Remote sites can add transport and site costs. Ask for a quote for your plot, or try the Build & Price tool for a first estimate.

Can a builder in Lombok buy just the frame?

Yes. Infinity Structural supplies engineered frame kits to builders and developers in Bali and Lombok, either supply-only or supply-and-erect. Builders can see the frame kit page or write to structural@ilo.net.au.

What should I check on my plot before ordering?

Check truck access, space to unload and store panels, lifting and assembly space, and power and water on site. Roads, unloading and lifting all affect the delivery and erection plan.
