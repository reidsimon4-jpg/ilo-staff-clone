How much does it cost to build a villa in Bali with steel framing?

What a steel frame villa costs at each build stage, in plain words.

Most Bali villa owners want one number before anything else: what will it cost per square metre? This article gives the prices ILO publishes for steel frame villas, explains what each price includes in plain words, and shows how to get a figure for your own plot.

If steel framing is new to you, read What is LGS? first. LGS is light gauge steel, a frame made from thin galvanised steel sections.

The short answer

ILO prices a steel frame villa by build stage. You pay for the stage you need.

  • Frame and roof: from about Rp 2.5 million per square metre.
  • Lockup shell: about Rp 4 million per square metre.
  • Turnkey villa: typically Rp 7-11 million per square metre, depending on finish level and location.

These are published starting figures. Your own price depends on the design, the floor area, the number of floors and the finishes you choose.

Why price by build stage?

A villa is built in layers. First the structure, then the weather-tight outer shell, then everything inside. Some owners want ILO to do all of it. Others have a builder they trust and only need the frame. Pricing by stage lets you compare like with like and decide where to start.

Stage 1: frame and roof (from about Rp 2.5 million/m2)

This is the engineered steel structure. A complete light gauge steel system has wall frames, floor sections and roof trusses. ILO makes them in its own factory in Banyuwangi, Java, and ships them to your site.

This stage suits owners and builders who already have a team for the rest of the job. It is also the lowest-risk way to try steel framing. If you are a builder who only needs the frame, look at Infinity Structural frame kits, which are sold for builders and developers in Bali and Lombok.

Stage 2: lockup shell (about Rp 4 million/m2)

A lockup shell is weather-tight. The structure is up, the roof is on and the building is enclosed, so you can secure it. The inside is still to be finished.

This is a common choice for owners who want to choose their own interior designer, joinery and fittings. It also lets you spread cost over time, because the finishing work can start when you are ready.

Stage 3: turnkey villa (typically Rp 7-11 million/m2)

Turnkey means complete. ILO handles the build through to finishes and services and hands over a finished villa. In the ILO process, the last step is finishing and handover: cladding, services and finishes, then a final inspection with quality documents.

The range is wide because finish level and location change the cost. Stone, joinery, bathrooms, kitchens and fittings are all choices. A simple, clean finish sits near the bottom of the range. A high-end finish sits near the top. Read our build process to see each step.

Why the total can still be lower than concrete

Total project cost with LGS is generally 25-40% lower than traditional concrete and brick, once speed and waste savings are included. Three things drive that:

  • Less labour. The frame is made in a factory, so there is less work on site.
  • A shorter build. Projects are typically completed 2 to 3 times faster. You pay for fewer months of site costs and you start earning sooner.
  • Less waste. Site waste is about 2% with LGS, compared with about 20% for conventional methods.

For a side-by-side look, see LGS vs traditional block work in Bali.

What to ask before you compare quotes

Two quotes can look far apart and still be for different things. Ask each builder the same questions.

  • Which stage does the price cover: frame and roof, lockup shell or turnkey?
  • What is the finish level, and which items are allowed for and which are extra?
  • Is the price per square metre of floor area, and are verandas, pools and landscaping counted?
  • Who is responsible for the design, the permits and the engineering?

ILO confirms exactly what is included when it issues a quote for your project.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a steel frame villa cost per square metre in Bali?

ILO prices by build stage. An engineered frame and roof package starts at about Rp 2.5 million per square metre. A weather-tight lockup shell is about Rp 4 million per square metre. A complete turnkey villa is typically Rp 7-11 million per square metre, depending on finish level and location.

Is a steel frame villa cheaper than a concrete villa in Bali?

Total project cost is generally 25-40% lower than traditional concrete and brick once speed and waste savings are counted. The saving comes from less labour, a shorter build and about 2% site waste compared with about 20% for conventional methods.

What is the difference between a lockup shell and a turnkey villa?

A lockup shell is weather-tight. The frame, roof and outer enclosure are in place, so you can secure the building and finish the inside separately. A turnkey villa is complete, with finishes and services done, and ready to hand over.

Why is there such a wide range for a turnkey villa?

Finish level and location move the price. Stone, joinery, bathrooms, kitchens and fittings vary widely in cost from one villa to the next. That is why a turnkey price is a range and not a single figure.

How do I get a price for my own villa?

Use the Build and Price tool on the ILO home page for an instant estimate based on building type, build stage, floor area and number of floors. Then send the result to ILO for an exact quote.
