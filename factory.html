Precision LGS Production in East Java

Our dedicated factory in Banyuwangi, East Java operates with a highly skilled team to produce precision-engineered light gauge steel components. Every product manufactured meets our rigorous quality standards, ensuring structural integrity for modular construction projects across Indonesia.
