The Eco-Friendly Future of Real Estate

Why sustainable construction with Light Gauge Steel is the new standard.

As the Indonesian real estate market evolves, sustainability is moving from a buzzword to a regulatory and market requirement. Developers are increasingly turning to Light Gauge Steel (LGS) to drastically reduce the environmental impact of their projects.

100% Recyclable Materials

Unlike concrete, which is highly carbon-intensive and difficult to recycle, steel is the world's most recycled material. A LGS frame can be completely melted down and repurposed at the end of a building's lifecycle without losing its structural properties.

Zero On-Site Waste

Traditional construction sites generate massive amounts of waste, from off-cuts to damaged materials. Because our modular components are fabricated with exact precision in our Java factory, they arrive on-site ready to assemble, virtually eliminating material waste and reducing trips to the landfill.

Energy Efficiency

The space within LGS stud walls allows for superior insulation and thermal bridging solutions. This results in villas and commercial buildings that require significantly less energy to cool in Indonesia's tropical climate, lowering carbon footprints long after construction is finished.
