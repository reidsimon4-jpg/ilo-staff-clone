From factory to keys: the ILO build timeline

The eight steps of an ILO build, from first call to handover.

A steel frame build runs differently from a block and concrete build. Much of the work moves from the site into a factory. This article walks through the eight steps ILO uses, from the first conversation to the day you get the keys, and explains where the time is saved.

We do not quote fixed weeks for each step here. The length of a project depends on its size, finish level and site. What we can say is that steel framing is usually finished two to three times faster than traditional methods, and the reasons are in the steps below.

The idea: build in two places at once

In a traditional build, the site goes through one stage after another. Foundations first, then walls, then roof. Each stage waits for the one before it, and rain can stop the work.

With ILO, the frame is made in a factory while the site is being prepared. Both jobs run at the same time. When the frames arrive, the foundation is ready and the crew can start assembling.

The eight steps

1. Consultation and planning

You tell ILO about the site, the budget and the design you want. ILO checks whether the project suits steel framing and outlines the scope. If you want a price before you talk, the Build & Price tool gives an instant estimate.

2. Architectural and structural design

Engineers draw the building in CAD and design the frame around Indonesia's seismic needs and tropical climate. This is where the plan is fixed, so changes are cheapest here.

3. Permits and approvals

Indonesia has building permit and compliance requirements. ILO's team works through them as part of the project so that you can focus on the design and the budget.

4. Factory manufacturing

Galvanised steel coil is cold-formed into C-sections, tracks and studs, cut to size under computer control. The work happens at ILO's factory in Banyuwangi, Java, so weather does not slow it.

5. Frame prefabrication

The parts are assembled into complete wall frames, floor systems and roof trusses in the factory. Checking the work indoors reduces errors on site.

6. Transport and logistics

The frames are packed and shipped from Banyuwangi to the site, whether in Bali, Lombok, Jakarta or elsewhere in Indonesia.

7. On-site assembly

The crew erects wall panels, floor systems and roof trusses on the prepared foundation. This is the stage where steel framing saves the most time. A villa superstructure that takes months in concrete can be erected in days.

8. Finishing and handover

Cladding, internal linings, electrical, plumbing and other services, and finishes go in. ILO then carries out a final inspection and hands the project over with documentation.

Where the time is saved

  • Overlap: the frame is made while the foundation is prepared.
  • Factory conditions: computer-controlled cutting and assembly are not held up by weather.
  • Fast erection: prefabricated frames arrive ready to assemble, so the superstructure goes up in days.
  • Less waste: about 2% material waste on a steel frame build, against about 20% for traditional work, so there is less to clear and rework.

What you can do to keep the timeline short

Decide the design and finish level early, and make changes before the structural design is final. After the frame is made, changes cost more. Have the site access for delivery trucks checked as well. Frequently asked questions

How long does an ILO build take?

A steel frame build is usually finished two to three times faster than traditional methods. Because the frame is made in the factory while the site is prepared, the structure of a villa can be erected in days. The full programme depends on the size of the building, the finish level and the site, so ILO confirms it after the design stage.

Where are the frames made?

In ILO's own factory in Banyuwangi, Java. Frames are shipped from there to Bali, Lombok, Jakarta and other parts of Indonesia.

Who handles building permits?

Permits and regulatory compliance are one of the eight steps in the ILO process. ILO's team works through Indonesia's permit requirements as part of the project.

Can I start the foundation before the frame is finished?

Yes. This is one of the main reasons the programme is shorter. The frame is made in the factory while the site team prepares the ground and foundation, so the two jobs overlap.

What do I receive at handover?

A final quality inspection and project handover with documentation, after cladding, internal linings, services and finishes are complete.
