Fire Safety in a Steel Frame Villa in Bali

What a non-combustible frame does in a fire, what still burns, and what to ask your builder.

Fire is a risk in every villa, from kitchens and electrics to candles and cigarettes. Villa owners often ask whether a steel frame is safer than the timber or concrete they know. This article explains what a light gauge steel (LGS) frame does in a fire, what it does not do, and what to ask your builder. It uses ILO's published information only.

Steel does not burn

LGS is non-combustible. ILO states that it does not ignite, burn or add fuel to a fire. That is the main fire advantage over timber, which is a fuel. In a timber-framed villa, the walls and the roof frame can burn along with everything else. In a steel frame, the structure itself gives the fire nothing to feed on.

What still burns

A non-combustible frame does not make a villa fireproof. Furniture, curtains, bedding, kitchen contents and many finishes can still burn. Roof coverings, insulation and wall linings differ in how they behave. Fire performance is a result of the whole wall, ceiling and roof build-up, not the frame alone. If fire safety matters to you, ask your builder and engineer what linings and insulation are in the design.

Electrical faults and the steel frame

Electrical faults are a common cause of building fires. ILO notes that wiring in an LGS frame passes through pre-punched service holes. If a wire fault touches the conductive steel, it trips the circuit breaker immediately. This is a benefit of steel, but it does not replace good installation. Cables must be correctly installed, protected and earthed by a licensed electrician. For how services are run, read wiring, plumbing and air-con in a steel frame villa.

Roofs

Steel roof trusses are non-combustible and are not eaten by termites. They are made in the factory to the engineered drawings. See steel roof trusses for Bali villas for more.

Insurance

ILO's published guidance is that the non-combustible nature of steel often reduces insurance premiums for property developers. This is a general point, not a promise. Every insurer in Indonesia sets its own terms. If insurance cost matters to your investment, ask an insurer for a quote on a steel frame villa and compare it with a timber or block alternative. For an investor view, see building a rental villa in Bali with a steel frame.

Other safety points a steel frame helps with

  • Termites: steel is not a food source, so no chemical soil treatment is needed.
  • Mould and rot: steel is inorganic and cannot support mould or rot.
  • Earthquakes: the frame is much lighter than concrete and flexes rather than cracking. Read steel frame vs concrete in Bali's earthquake zone.

Questions to ask any builder about fire

  • What are the wall and ceiling linings, and what do they do in a fire?
  • Is the roof covering and insulation fire-rated, and who says so?
  • Who installs and tests the wiring and the earthing?
  • Can I see how the villa meets local building rules?
  • What did my insurer say about this construction type?

Frequently asked questions

Does a steel frame burn?

No. Light gauge steel is non-combustible. It does not ignite, burn or add fuel to a fire. Furniture, finishes and contents inside the villa can still burn.

Is a steel frame villa fireproof?

No building is fireproof. Steel adds no fuel, which is a real safety gain. How the whole wall, ceiling and roof perform in a fire also depends on the linings and finishes chosen, so ask your engineer for the design.

Can a steel frame lower my insurance premium?

It can. ILO's published guidance is that non-combustible steel often reduces insurance premiums for property developers. Insurers set their own terms, so ask for a quote on a steel frame before you build.

Is the electrical wiring safe in a steel frame?

Wiring runs through pre-punched holes in the frame. ILO notes that if an electrical fault touches the conductive steel, it trips the circuit breaker immediately. Wiring still has to be installed and earthed correctly by a licensed electrician.

Is a steel roof frame better than timber for fire?

A steel roof truss does not burn, while a timber roof frame can. Roofs also carry termite and moisture risks in timber, which steel avoids.
